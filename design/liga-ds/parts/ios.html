Live match activity

A live match starts a Live Activity. On the lock screen it shows both players, the score, a race bar to 10 and a Cheer button. On the Dynamic Island it sits compact (your avatar, live score), drops to minimal when another activity is running, and expands on long press. The island resizes with the ease in out curve, no bounce.
