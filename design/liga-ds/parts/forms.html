Date, time and sliders

Scheduling a match or ending a season uses an inline month grid, never a system wheel for the date. Past days are greyed out and cannot be picked, times are a short list of chips with taken slots struck out. Sliders always show their value in a bubble above the thumb.

    Compact selects in a toolbar

    On phones a compact select opens a bottom sheet with the same options. A filter that differs from the default turns solid, so you always see what is on.

    Do
    • Show recent searches before anything is typed
    • Underline the part that matched
    • Keep the menu the same width as its field
    Don't
    • Use a native select for players, it hides colours and ratings
    • Hide locked options, explain them instead
    • Blame the spelling in the empty state

    Swipe, refresh, load more

    Rows in your own match history swipe left to reveal two quick actions. Every action also lives in the long press menu, so swiping is never the only way. Feeds refresh with a pull from the top and load older items with a row at the bottom.

    Swipe action row

    • D7 DaveLost 6 to 10, 2 h ago −12
    • M5 MiraWon 10 to 8, yesterday +16
    • SK Sam and Kim2v2 with Ann, 3 days ago +9
    • J3 JoeWon 10 to 2, 5 h ago +7
    Long press for every actionHold a row for half a second, right click it, or focus it and press Enter. The menu opens next to the row.

    Swipe left or press the left arrow to reveal Rematch and Delete. Right arrow or Escape closes.

    Matches older than 24 h cannot be deleted, so the 2v2 row only shows Rematch. Delete always offers Undo for 6 s.
