Your device asks for reduced motion, so these demos finish instantly.

Do
  • Use the token durations and var(--ease) for every transition.
  • Animate transform and opacity of neutral surfaces, keep it at 60 fps.
  • Let one thing lead. The rest follows with a short stagger.
Don't
  • Use springs, overshoot or bounce, even in celebrations.
  • Fade a brand or meaning colour to make it lighter.
  • Make the user wait on a scene to keep going.
