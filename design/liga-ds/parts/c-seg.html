Segmented control and tabs

A segmented control switches a view in place, 2 to 4 options of equal width with centred labels. Tabs split a screen into sections, like a profile, and may scroll. Both move one solid indicator, 280 ms ease in out.

    • Give every segment the same width and centre the label.

    Don't
    • Stretch a segmented control across a wide screen, cap it at 420 px.
    • Put more than 4 options in a segmented control.
    • Use a segmented control to navigate to another screen.
