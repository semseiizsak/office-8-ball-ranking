Share and invite

Share cards come in three formats, story, square and link, with a fixed palette so they look the same in both themes. The invite gives three ways in: scan, link, or code, and the person invited lands on a clear join card.

Share cards

Cards use the fixed palette, so they look the same in light and dark. The Liga wordmark sits bottom left on every format.
