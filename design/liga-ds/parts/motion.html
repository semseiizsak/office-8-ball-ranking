Motion tokens

Five durations and one curve cover everything. Pick the shortest one that still reads. Scenes are the only place that may run longer than 400 ms.

One curve, ease in out. --ease: cubic-bezier(.65, 0, .35, 1). Slow start, fast middle, slow end. It never goes past 1, so nothing overshoots or settles back. No spring and no bounce curves anywhere, also not in Expo (use Easing.bezier(.65, 0, .35, 1)).

TokenValueUsed for
--d-press120 msButton and row press, scale to .97 and back
--d-fast180 msHover, colour change, toast in and out, unread dot, reduced motion fades
--d-base280 msToggle, segmented indicator, tab bar slide, sheet up, list rows, skeleton crossfade, reaction pop
--d-slow400 msRank swap, progress fill, crown drop, anything that travels far
--d-scene1200 msCelebration scenes, the longest step, the whole scene ends by 1.4 s
--mo-count800 msNumber count up for rating, coins and bounty
--mo-stagger40 msDelay between rows or tiles appearing
--mo-rise8 pxHow far things rise when they appear
--ease.65, 0, .35, 1Every animation, no exceptions

Reduced motion

When the phone asks for reduced motion, nothing moves, scales or turns. The final state appears with one fade on --d-base. Numbers show their final value, confetti and flashes are skipped. Use the preview switch at the top of this group to see it.
