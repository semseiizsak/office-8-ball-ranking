Choice cards

When each option needs a sentence. Three across on wide screens, stacked on a phone.
Do
  • Make the whole row tappable and put the control at the end.
  • Say why a control is disabled in the row text.
  • Use a toggle only when the change applies at once.
Don't
  • Use a toggle in a form that needs a Save button, use a checkbox.
  • Use radios for two options, use a segmented control.
  • Rely on colour alone, the thumb and tick move as well.
