Rules

Do
  • Use only semantic tokens in components
  • Use colour solid, with its fixed text partner
  • Keep one primary button per screen
  • Keep touch targets at least 44 px
  • Give every icon button an accessible name
  • Use ease in out, no overshoot
  • Show the stakes before a match is played
Don't
  • Tint or fade the brand or meaning colours
  • Use red for a loss
  • Use pale pills with a coloured border
  • Use italics or text shadows
  • Put a small label above a title
  • Space out uppercase letters
  • Use dashes, dots or slashes to separate text, use gap or a line break
  • Draw sport objects in the core UI
