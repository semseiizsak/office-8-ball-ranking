Bottom sheet

Log a match, send a challenge, filter a list. Swipe down, tap the scrim or close to dismiss.

  • Radius24 top
  • HeightFits content, max 88%
  • ActionsOne primary at the bottom
Dialog

Only for steps that cannot be undone. The safe choice is always one tap away.

  • Width360 max
  • ActionsTwo, stacked
  • Scrim tapCancels
Action menu

Secondary actions for one item. Destructive goes last, after a divider, in red.

  • Rows44 tall, up to 6
  • Radius16, items 8
  • ScrimNone, tap outside
Popover

A short tip that points at what it explains. Never holds a form or a choice that matters.

  • Width280 max
  • TextTwo lines, one action
  • DismissTap anywhere
Do
  • Keep the screen behind visible, so people know where they are
  • Put the main action at the bottom, in thumb reach
  • Say exactly what will happen: "Delete match", not "OK"
Don't
  • Stack a sheet on a sheet, or open a dialog from a menu just to confirm sharing
  • Use a dialog for a form or a choice of more than two
  • Hide the only way to close behind a swipe
