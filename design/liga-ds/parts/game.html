Every match, quest and reaction earns XP. Levels are hexagons in solid brand, milestones every ten levels turn gold. The bar fills with a green gain chunk first, then the brand fill catches up; crossing the top flashes the whole card brand for one beat, never a bounce.

Season pass and divisions

A season runs a quarter. XP from matches climbs a 30 tier pass with a free row for everyone and a Pro row on top. Divisions group players by rating each week: the top two go up, the bottom two go down, the zones are marked with solid green and red, never with tinted rows.
