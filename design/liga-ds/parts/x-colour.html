Colour

Three layers. Primitives are raw values, semantic tokens say what a value is for and swap with the theme, components only read semantic tokens. Neutrals carry almost every screen, one violet accent marks the brand, and meaning colours stay rare so a win or a live match pops. Every colour is used solid, never tinted or transparent.
