Pills and chips

Pills show state and are never tappable. Chips are tappable filters with a 44 px hit area. Both are solid: a meaning or an active state is a solid fill with contrasting text, never a pale tint with a coloured border.

Don't
  • Make a pill tappable. If it does something, it is a chip or a button.

  • Use meaning colours for decoration, green always means a win.
